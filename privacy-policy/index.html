This Privacy Policy explains how HaiMeet (“we,” “us,” or “our”), a product of HaldarAi, collects, uses, and shares information when you use the HaiMeet Android app (package name com.HaldarAi.HaiMeet, the “App”) and the HaiMeet video meeting service at https://HaiMeet.vercel.app (the “Service”) that the App displays.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Section 01

Information We Collect

How the App Works

The App opens the Service inside a secure in-app browser window. Meetings and their features run on the Service, so information you provide while using it is handled by the Service as described in this Policy.

Personal Information

The App itself does not collect your name, email address, or phone number. If the Service asks you for details such as a display name or meeting ID so you can join a meeting, we use them only to run that meeting.

Audio and Video

When you join a call and allow access, your camera and microphone streams are sent in real time to the other people in your meeting through the Service. The App itself does not record or save your audio or video on your device.

Usage and Technical Data

When the App connects to the Service, certain technical information is received automatically by the Service and its hosting provider, including your IP address, device model, Android version, in-app browser (WebView) version, language setting, the date and time of access, and diagnostic or error information.

Device Permissions

The App requests only the permissions it needs to run video meetings:

📷
Asked at runtime

Camera

Lets you share your video in a meeting. Used only while a call is using video.

🎤
Asked at runtime

Microphone

Lets other participants hear you. Used only while a call is using audio.

🌐
Granted automatically

Internet Access

Needed to load the Service and connect your calls.

📶
Granted automatically

Network State

Lets the App see whether you are online, so calls can adapt to your connection.

🔊
Granted automatically

Audio Settings

Lets the App adjust audio routing during calls, for example switching between speaker and earpiece.

Camera and microphone access is granted only to the HaiMeet Service and to no other website. You can review or withdraw it at any time in Android Settings › Apps › HaiMeet › Permissions.

What the App Does Not Access

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, files, SMS, or call logs, and it does not use your advertising ID.

Advertising and Analytics

The App does not show ads and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and maintain the App and the Service, including connecting your audio and video calls.
  • Keep the App working well, by diagnosing errors and adjusting calls to your network connection.
  • Protect the App and its users, by detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, and security problems.
  • Respond to your questions, requests, and complaints.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requests.
Section 03

Disclosure of Your Information

We may share information in these situations:

  • With other meeting participants: Your audio, video, and any name or details you enter are visible to the people in your meeting.
  • With service providers: Companies that host the Service (such as Vercel) and provide the infrastructure that connects calls process technical data on our behalf.
  • For legal reasons: When required by law, court order, or a valid request from a public authority, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of HaiMeet, our users, or others.
  • For business transfers: In connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition of all or part of our business.
  • With your consent: For any other purpose, when you agree to it.
Section 04

Retention and Deletion

The live audio and video streams of a call are passed between participants while the call is in progress, and the App does not store them.

Like any in-app browser, the App may keep cached files, cookies, and local storage on your device so the Service loads faster and remembers your settings. You can remove this at any time in Android Settings › Apps › HaiMeet › Storage › Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.

Technical logs held by the Service and its hosting provider are kept only as long as needed for security, diagnostics, and legal obligations. To ask us to delete information we hold about you, contact our Grievance Officer in Section 10.

Section 05

Security of Your Information

The App connects to the Service over HTTPS, blocks insecure (non-HTTPS) content, and passes camera and microphone access only to the HaiMeet Service. While we work to protect your information with commercially reasonable measures, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 06

Your Rights and Choices

You are in control of how the App uses your device:

  • Permissions: Allow or deny camera and microphone access when asked, and change your choice later in Android Settings.
  • Local data: Clear the App’s stored data or uninstall the App at any time.
  • Your information: Ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, and withdraw any consent you gave us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, including India’s Information Technology Act, 2000 and Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To use any of these rights or to make a complaint, contact our Grievance Officer in Section 10.

Section 07

Links to Other Websites

Links inside the Service that lead to other websites open in your device’s browser or another app, outside of HaiMeet. We have no control over, and take no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Section 08

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a person under 18 is treated as a child, so if you are under 18 please use the App only with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without the required parental consent, we will delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the “Last Updated” date. For significant changes, we will also show a notice in the App or on the Service before they take effect.

Please review this Policy periodically. Changes are effective when they are posted on this page.
