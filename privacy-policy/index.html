This Privacy Policy describes how HaiMeet ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application, HaiMeet (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We collect various types of information in connection with the services we provide, including:

Personal Information

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number through the App. However, certain third-party services integrated into our App may collect such information as described below.

Usage Data

When you access the App, we may automatically collect certain information including, but not limited to, your device's IP address, browser type and version, the pages of our App you visit, the time and date of your visit, time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

Device Permissions

To provide our services, the App requires certain permissions on your device:

📷

Camera

Required for video conferencing. Accessed only during active video calls.

🎤

Microphone (Audio)

Required for audio/video calls. Accessed only during active calls.

📶

Network State

Used to determine connectivity and optimize call quality and data usage.

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Our App integrates Google AdMob for advertising purposes. AdMob may collect and use information about your device and usage to provide personalized ads, including:

  • Advertising ID: A unique, user-resettable ID for advertising provided by your device's OS.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and language settings.
  • Usage Information: Interactions with advertisements and the App.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our App, including monitoring its usage.
  • To manage your account and provide access to App functionalities.
  • To contact you via email, SMS, or other electronic communication regarding updates, security alerts, or contracted services.
  • To provide you with news, special offers, and general information about similar goods and services, unless you have opted out.
  • For data analysis, identifying usage trends, evaluating promotional campaigns, and improving our App, products, and services.
Section 03

Disclosure of Your Information

We may share your personal information in the following situations:

  • With Service Providers: To monitor and analyze App usage, display advertisements, and support our services.
  • For Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale, financing, or acquisition of all or part of our business.
  • With Affiliates: Shared with affiliates who are required to honor this Privacy Policy.
  • With Business Partners: To offer you certain products, services, or promotions.
  • With Other Users: When you share information publicly within the App, it may be viewed by all users.
  • With Your Consent: For any other purpose with your explicit consent.
Section 04

Retention of Your Personal Information

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. Retention continues to the extent required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Usage Data is generally retained for a shorter period, except where it is used to strengthen security, improve functionality, or where we are legally obligated to retain it for longer.

Section 05

Security of Your Personal Information

The security of your Personal Information is important to us. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 06

Links to Other Websites

Our App may contain links to other websites not operated by us. If you click a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites.

Section 07

Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us immediately.

If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from anyone under 13 without parental consent verification, we will take steps to remove that information from our servers.

Section 08

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and update the "Last Updated" date. We will also notify you via email and/or a prominent in-app notice prior to any change becoming effective.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes are effective when posted on this page.
